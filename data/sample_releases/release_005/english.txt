Lieutenant General Arjun Suresh Rao conducted the Annual Operational Review on 26 November 2026. The review assessed 200 senior officers spread across 4 battalions and 8 regiments. Key performance indicators from the past 12 months were rigorously evaluated. The review concluded with a 2-hour strategic briefing attended by all formation commanders. Lieutenant General Rao highlighted that operational efficiency had improved by 15 percent compared to the previous year and outlined priorities for the coming cycle.
