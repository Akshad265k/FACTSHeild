Colonel Meera Shankar Singh, Commandant of the Nashik Training Centre, conducted a 5-day training exercise in Nashik from 10 March 2026 to 14 March 2026. The exercise involved 180 cadets from the Ahmednagar and Nashik cantonments. General Rajesh Kumar Verma, General Officer Commanding, Southern Command, attended the closing ceremony at the Nashik Military Station on 14 March 2026. A total of 12 training scenarios were successfully completed during the exercise. Colonel Singh stated that the exercise improved operational readiness across all participating battalions in the Pune region.
