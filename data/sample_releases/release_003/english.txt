Dr. Priya Suresh Mehta, Chief Medical Officer, organised a community health awareness programme in Kolhapur on 20 July 2026. The programme reached 300 families across Kolhapur and Sangli districts. Lieutenant General Arjun Suresh Rao, Commander of the Western Division, inaugurated the event at the Kolhapur Military Hospital. A team of 8 specialist doctors participated in the programme. Dr. Mehta stated that the initiative successfully screened 450 individuals and announced expansion plans for Solapur and Satara districts.
