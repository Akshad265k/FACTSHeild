Colonel Meera Shankar Singh, Commandant of the Base Ordnance Depot, presided over an Independence Day ceremony on 15 August 2026. She presented commendation medals to 75 outstanding personnel who had demonstrated exceptional service. The event celebrated 7 battalions for their contributions to national defence. The ceremony was attended by 300 officers and jawans from the regional command. Colonel Singh emphasised the importance of dedication and teamwork in fulfilling the nation's defence commitments.
