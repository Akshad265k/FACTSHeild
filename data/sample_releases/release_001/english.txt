Brigadier Aditya Kumar Nair, Commander of the Southern Brigade, organised a public outreach programme in Pune on 15 September 2026. The programme was conducted across Pune and Satara districts with the participation of 250 Army personnel. Dr. Priya Suresh Mehta, Chief Medical Officer, led the medical camp component which served 120 families. The initiative covered 3 community centres and concluded with an awareness session at the Pune Military Station. Brigadier Nair commended the efforts of all participating units and announced plans for similar programmes in Nashik.
