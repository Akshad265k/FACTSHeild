SLIDE 2: THE THREAT MATRIX & OPERATIONAL NEED (Why LLMs Fail in Defense)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]: The Tactical Problem: Seams in Multilingual Communications
[Key Talking Points]:
1. The Translation Seam:
   - Official press statements released in English are translated to regional vernaculars (Hindi, Marathi, etc.).
   - Minor errors (e.g., swapping troop count 250 -> 2500, or altering location "Pune" -> "Mumbai") create panic, compromise OPSEC, or provide fodder for enemy propaganda wings.
2. The Disinformation Weapon:
   - Adversaries crop, alter, or subtly edit circulating digital copies to claim casualties, altered deployment dates, or false commanders.
3. Why Generative AI / Large Language Models (LLMs) Cannot Be Trusted:
   - Stochastic & Non-deterministic: The same prompt can produce different outputs.
   - Hallucinations: Dangerous confabulations in high-stakes military contexts.
   - Air-Gap & Compute Limits: Requires heavy GPU infrastructure and cannot guarantee zero data leakage.
   - The FACTSHIELD Difference: Pure rule-based, regex-pattern, transliteration, and cryptographic matching with ZERO black-box uncertainty.

SLIDE 4: EXTRACTION & NORMALIZATION ENGINE (Deep-Dive)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]: Deterministic Fact Extraction: Zero Hallucination
[Technical Breakdown]:
1. Numeric Normalization:
   - Full bi-directional conversion between Latin numerals (0-9) and Devanagari numerals (०-९).
   - Range preservation, troop strength recognition, and casualty figures.
2. Temporal Normalization:
   - Multi-format parsing: "15 September 2026", "15-09-2026", "१५ सप्टेंबर २०२६".
   - Converted to immutable ISO-8601 representation (2026-09-15) for language-independent equality testing.
3. Military Personnel & Rank Parsing:
   - Regex-driven Military Rank Prefixes: General, Lt Gen, Maj Gen, Brig, Col, Lt Col, Capt, etc.
   - Hybrid fallback: Detects unknown personnel via rank structures + Known entity alias graphs.
4. Geographic & Cantonment Matching:
   - Gazetteer of strategic bases, cantonments, operational commands, and district headquarters.
   - Cross-script phonetic mapping: "पुणे" == "Pune", "जोधपूर" == "Jodhpur", "खडकी" == "Kirkee".

SLIDE 5: OPSEC SENSITIVITY & CRITICALITY SCORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]: OPSEC Intelligence: Automated Criticality Classification
[Details]:
Not all facts carry equal tactical weight. FACTSHIELD automatically assigns criticality levels and operational security tags based on context:

- CRITICAL (Red):
  * Tags: TROOP_STRENGTH, CASUALTY_COUNTS, STRATEGIC_ASSETS
  * Impact: Direct operational vulnerability if misreported or altered.
- HIGH (Orange):
  * Tags: OPERATIONAL_TIMING, SENSITIVE_LOCATION, MOVEMENT_DATE
  * Impact: Inconsistencies could telegraph tactical schedules or deployment vectors.
- MEDIUM (Yellow):
  * Tags: PERSONNEL_IDENTITY, VISITING_DIGNITARY, UNIT_DESIGNATION
  * Impact: Public relations seam or misattribution risk.
- LOW (Green):
  * Tags: CIVIC_ACTIVITY, GENERAL_ADMINISTRATION, ROUTINE_WELFARE

Result: Command authorities immediately see WHICH discrepancies pose active security threats versus typographical slips.

SLIDE 6: PROVENANCE VERIFICATION & ANTI-TAMPER ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]: Provenance Tracking: Digital Fingerprinting Against Factual Drift
[Operational Concept]:
1. Authoritative Release Fingerprinting:
   - Upon release approval, the system extracts all canonical facts and compiles a deterministic, order-independent SHA-256 cryptographic digest.
   - Example Fingerprint: `a3f9c0e29b18d...`
2. Digital Circulation Verification:
   - When a copy of the release surfaces on social media, foreign blogs, or messaging channels, it is fed into FACTSHIELD.
   - System recalculates the circulating fingerprint and scans for fact drift.
3. Actionable Diff Presentation:
   - Eliminates convoluted line-by-line git diffs.
   - Presents facts in clear, intelligence-ready operational alerts:
     * Location: `Pune -> Mumbai` [CHANGED] (Tactical Alert)
     * Numbers: `250 -> 2500` [CHANGED] (Disinformation Alert)
     * Names: `Brigadier Nair` [REMOVED]
